Our Story

From a Small Idea to a Global Platform

Founded in 2018 by a group of gallery owners and event producers, Stackly began as a simple solution to a frustrating problem: booking exhibition stalls was unnecessarily complicated.

Today, we partner with 180+ venues across 35 countries, serving over 50,000 visitors annually. Our platform streamlines the entire process — from discovering exhibitions to confirming stall reservations — so exhibitors can focus on what truly matters: their craft.
