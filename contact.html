FAQ

Common Questions, Answered.

A few things exhibitors and visitors commonly ask before getting in touch.

How can I book an exhibition stall?

Visit our Book a Stall page, select your exhibition, choose your preferred stall package and complete the booking details.

Can I request a specific stall location?

Stall availability and placement depend on the selected exhibition and package. Contact our team with your requirements and we can assist you.

Can I change my booking details?

Contact our support team with your booking details. We'll help you understand the available options for making changes.

Do you provide additional exhibition services?

Selected exhibitions may offer additional services such as photography, catering, AV equipment and connectivity packages.

How quickly will I receive a response?

Our team reviews enquiries during office hours and will respond as soon as possible.

Can companies discuss partnership opportunities?

Yes. Select Partnership Opportunity in the contact form and provide some information about your proposal.
